## Summary
Restores the vibrant Glassmorphism aesthetic by removing opaque background layers that were hiding the wave mesh, and dramatically enhances card reactivity.

## Changes
- Removed opaque Tailwind `bg-slate-50` and `dark:bg-slate-900` classes from the `<body>` to allow the wave mesh to shine through.
- Added explicit base `background-color` to `.wave-background` in `style.css` so it blends properly on transparent bodies.
- Enhanced card classes with deeper `backdrop-blur-2xl`, stronger `hover:-translate-y-1` physics, and increased transparency (`bg-white/20`) to maximize the glass effect.
